I gave Google's open medical AI model five medical images with known answers. It got four right, and the one it got wrong is the most useful part.

MedGemma 1.5 (4B parameters) ran on a single free cloud GPU and read public-domain images:
✅ Normal chest X-ray: "Normal chest X-ray." (9 s)
✅ Healthy retina: no diabetic retinopathy, no referral
✅ Diabetic retina: microaneurysms and haemorrhages, refer to a specialist
✅ Melanoma: ABCDE checklist, melanoma first, biopsy recommended
❌ Lobar pneumonia X-ray: "No acute cardiopulmonary process."

That last answer is a clean, confident, wrong report. That's why a model like this is a fast first reader, not a doctor.
