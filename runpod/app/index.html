The problem

Imaging volume keeps growing faster than the number of specialists who read it. Studies wait in a queue, and urgent ones can sit behind routine ones. A model can write a first draft and flag what looks abnormal, so the specialist starts from a draft instead of a blank page. The specialist still decides.

The workflow

MedGemma readson a GPU that runs only during the session
Draft reportfindings, impression, referral flag
Human reviewthe clinician accepts, edits or rejects
